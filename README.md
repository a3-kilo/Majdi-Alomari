# Dr. Majdi M. Alomari

**Associate Professor of Electrical Engineering**  
Australian University · Kuwait

**PhD · FIEAust · CPEng · Senior Member IEEE**

An academic website presenting the research, publications, and professional profile of **Dr. Majdi M. Alomari**.

## Research

Dr. Alomari's work spans power systems and control, renewable energy, smart-grid technologies, and energy efficiency. His research explores electrical-network stability, emerging energy technologies, and approaches to more sustainable energy use.
