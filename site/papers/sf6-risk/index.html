SF₆ leakage risk in gas-insulated switchgear under the interaction of ageing and environmental stress

Majdi M. Alomari, Ahmed Bani-Mustafa, Abdel-Wahab B. Dirawieh, Nafesah I. Alshdaifat, Hania EL-Kanj, Ayse Topal, Badriyah Alhalaili

Results in Engineering · Vol. 29 · 109929

Read original publication

Research overview

Develops a Reliability–Environmental–Economic framework for estimating SF₆ leakage risk in Kuwait’s high-voltage gas-insulated switchgear, considering equipment ageing and environmental stresses, including heat, dust, salinity and humidity.
