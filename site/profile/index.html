Teaching

Courses taught include Power Systems, Power System Analysis, Smart Grid Technology, Electrical Machines, Control Systems, Electrical Circuits, Instrumentation and Measurement, and Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs).

Teaching has also included laboratory work in electrical machines, electronics, circuits, measurement and control.

Academic service

Service includes conference committees for IPST 2021, EADE 2024, ICACER 2026 and AU-ICDSA 2026, alongside journal reviewing or editorial activity for publications including IEEE Access and IET Generation, Transmission & Distribution.
